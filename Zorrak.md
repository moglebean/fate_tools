Backstory

Zorrak was hatched under the blazing red sun of the Ashen Wastes, a time and place his clan took as an omen of great destiny. From a young age, he was convinced he was more dragon than dragonborn — a mighty creature born in a mortal shell. His youth was filled with collecting shiny rocks and scorched scraps of parchment, convinced they were “treasures of his kind.”

As he grew older, Zorrak wandered far from his tribe in search of his “true dragonhood.” While his battle skills are undeniable, he has a tendency to roar dramatically in every confrontation, attempt to sleep on coins he can’t afford, and demand peasants treat him like royalty. Most people aren’t sure whether he’s a terrifying warrior or an eccentric madman — often both.

Stunts
